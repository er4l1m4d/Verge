How it works

What Verge is

Verge is a decision-support tool for Polymarket's hourly BTC Up/Down markets. It checks every hour whether there's a mathematical edge worth betting on, and tells you when to skip.

It is not a prediction engine. It's a filter — it watches the market and only speaks up when the odds look mispriced.

Reading the dashboard

The top shows the current market (BTC, hourly) and a countdown to when the hour closes. Below that is the big decision — BET UP, BET DOWN, or SKIP.

The three blocks below the decision show the evidence: what each indicator found and how much it contributed to the score. The odds bar shows the market price (white dot) versus what the indicators think the probability should be (amber dot) — the gap between them is the potential opportunity.

The bottom row shows your last 10 resolved trades and your running win rate.

The indicators

RSI Momentum — is the move exhausted or just starting?
Volume Activity — is this move backed by real trading?
MA Trend Direction — is price above or below its recent average?

BET vs SKIP

Verge doesn't bet every hour. It only bets when there's a measurable edge after fees. Skipping most of the time is how the tool protects you — it's not indecision, it's discipline.

How resolution works

Each hour closes at a set time. If BTC is higher than when the hour started, the "UP" side wins. If it's lower, the "DOWN" side wins. Verge logs the outcome and updates the win rate in real time.
